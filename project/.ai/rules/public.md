# Public

## Client-verified JA copy for About / Allergen / FAQ
The Japanese copy for the About, Allergen and FAQ sections is drafted and out for client review in `.context/ja-copy-review.md`. Do NOT implement those sections until it returns approved — the allergen and halal-certification wording makes factual claims that must be verified, and the client owns the certification details.

Two standing rules when writing JA copy for this project:
- Never invent facts from the English source. The EN copy contains unverifiable specifics (van year, supplier countries, certification bodies, ingredient provenance). Translate them faithfully and flag them for client verification instead of asserting them.
- Never invent kanji for a person's name. The founder names (Kenji Sato, Tariq Al-Mansoor) stay in Latin until the client supplies the correct Japanese spelling.
- Absolutes such as 「一切使用していない」「不使用」 carry legal weight for allergen labels in Japan. Soften to what is provably true rather than translating the English claim literally.
